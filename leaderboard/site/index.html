CANDI imputation leaderboard

Self-supervised epigenome imputation, scored against retrained rivals and naive baselines, with the 2019 ENCODE Imputation Challenge field as an anchor underneath. Every cell is addressed by six fields — method, regime, truth, panel, scope, metric — and a row missing any of them never enters the ranked table. Two regimes are live, eic.19→20,21,22 and eic.pilot→20,21,22: they differ only in the loci each method's transferable parameters were fit on, and both score chr20 + chr21 + chr22. The truth toggle measures the same rows a second time against the 2019 pipeline. Count-space and p-value-space numbers never share a column. The board measures missing-mark transfer within a cell type the method has seen — not unseen cell types.

Every row this page used to carry is void: the old board scored all 23 chromosomes, chr19 included, and chr19 is the chromosome CANDI trained on. The retrains are running. Nothing is ranked until the noise floor on the new panels is measured.
