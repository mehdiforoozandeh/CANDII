# f146 — Asked-for measurement with a calibrated reference, 3 seeds, 22 assays, 32…

Parent:: [[q79_do_the_final_minicandi_s_negative_binomi]]

## Answer

Almost as well as calibration allows: 90% interval coverage in the top 1% of observed bins is 0.669 against 0.710 for draws from the model itself, and 0.576 against 0.554 in the top 0.1%. Most of the apparent miss at large peaks is selection on the outcome. The real gap is where the model predicts its largest values: the truth falls below the lower bound in 6.2% of the top 1% predicted bins against 2.9% calibrated, the mean overshoot the EIC board shows. Recalibration changes neither.
