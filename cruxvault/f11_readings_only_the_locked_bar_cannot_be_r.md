## Answer

These knobs give the strongest covariate signal of the ten: in p, g beats its twin by a median 0.36–0.62 on trained pairs and 0.31–0.48 on never-trained pairs for control scaling, identity and depth; control depth carries over to new pairs in 16 of 16 cells.
