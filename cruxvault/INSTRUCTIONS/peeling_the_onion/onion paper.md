# onion paper

## Methods

### Methods: Data

Every paragraph and table in this section is a draft by the agent, from files that the run recorded. Each number names its source file. Source files are under `cruxvault/results/`; the index of every number is `cruxvault/results/methods_data_eval/sources.md`.

==**What we built.** The dataset is called `eic_v1`. It holds the tracks of the ENCODE Imputation Challenge (EIC), i.e. a public benchmark of 363 non-control tracks: 267 training, 45 validation and 51 blind tracks (`cruxvault/results/t273/AG2_LEAKAGE.md`). We rebuilt every track from the reads of its published ENCODE experiment. We did not reuse the challenge's processed files. Seven training DNase-seq tracks had a challenge file from another experiment. We rebuilt these seven from the published experiment's own FASTQs (`cruxvault/results/t170/build_plan.md`). The build list has 1,217 rows in four priorities (104, 292, 99 and 722 rows). `eic_v1` holds priorities 1 to 3, i.e. the validation tracks, the training tracks and the designed counterfactual views (`cruxvault/results/t170/check.txt`).==

==**Pipelines.** Each assay ran the standard ENCODE-DCC pipeline of that assay. Histone ChIP-seq and its controls ran chip-seq-pipeline2 v2.2.2. DNase-seq ran dnase-seq-pipeline v3.0.0. ATAC-seq ran atac-seq-pipeline v2.2.3 (`cruxvault/results/t183/fastq_cost.tsv`, column `pipeline`). The portal does not serve 129 FASTQs. These cover 46 experiments of six Roadmap tissue cells completely (6 validation tracks, 31 training tracks and 9 controls). For these 46 we started from ENCODE's redacted unfiltered GRCh38 alignments, i.e. BAM files with read positions but no read sequence. They get every alignment-level view and no FASTQ-level view (`cruxvault/results/t170/build_plan.md`). Controls come from each ChIP experiment's ENCODE control list: 75 control experiments, of which 63 control a training experiment (`cruxvault/results/t170/build_plan.md`).==

==**Views and halves.** A view is one way to process the reads of a track. The base view pools all reads. Three FASTQ-level views imitate other sequencing runs: `se` keeps mate 1 of a paired-end library, and `crop36` and `crop50` cut reads to 36 and 50 bp. Five alignment-level views change processing: three depth thinnings (`depth0.50`, `depth0.25`, `depth0.125`), no duplicate removal (`nodedup`), and no mapping-quality filter (`mapq0`, not for ATAC-seq) (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`). Each view has two pseudoreplicate halves, `pr1` and `pr2`, i.e. a random read-level split of the view into two disjoint halves. All 43 paired-end training experiments get the `se` view (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`). Among training experiments, `crop36` exists for 3 ATAC-seq, 14 broad-histone and 12 narrow-histone experiments, and `crop50` for 3, 14 and 10. DNase-seq has no crop view, because its training reads are 36 bp or shorter (`cruxvault/results/t170/run_type_coverage.tsv`).==

==**The freeze.** The table build started at 14:32 UTC on 2026-11-13 (Nibi job 27156918). It read 2,740 measurement directories and wrote 2,740 rows: 2,328 training, 334 validation and 78 blind rows. The 78 blind rows are the 7 counterfactual testbed tracks, for evaluation only. It also wrote 15,123 pairs, of which 102 are never-trained designed validation pairs. The `tables_sha256` is `a068a867...b135`. The dataset check printed `check: PASS` with every rule at 0 (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`). Table M1 below counts the tracks per assay (`cruxvault/results/methods_data_eval/dataset_table.tsv`).==

==**What is absent, and why.** The 21 absent tracks of priorities 1 to 3 were all queued or running at the freeze, and none failed verification. Their causes are as follows (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`, `cruxvault/results/t183/TAIL.md`). Seventeen tracks were slow in DNase-seq alignment: paired-end `bwa sampe` often needs more than one day, with up to 984.9 M reads per row. Two tracks ran out of memory in the Picard step of the pipeline. One paired-end library had no adapter record on the portal. One H3K9ac track hit a read-name format that breaks mate pairing. The tail is still running. Later rows go into update versions of the dataset, and no result of iterations 1 to 3 uses them. Three consequences follow for these results. First, DNase-seq is not imputation-scorable, because only 1 validation track has halves and the rule needs 3. Second, H3K9ac reaches 3 validation targets only through one replicate view, which has no signal-space score. Third, 22 of the 42 validation targets have no accessibility-only item, because their cell has no accessibility training track (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`). HAP-1 has no track in `eic_v1`.==

==**Cost.** PI ruling 3 asked for the alignment cost of the uniform base. Of the 376 experiments of priorities 1 to 3, 330 ran a FASTQ alignment; the other 46 are the redacted-alignment rows. The 330 used 4,511 CPU-h in all, with a median of 6.89 CPU-h per experiment. Slurm allocated 19,894 CPU-h, so the jobs used 4,511 / 19,894 = 23% of what they held. The CPU-h per million reads was 0.198 for chip-seq-pipeline2, 0.136 for dnase-seq-pipeline and 0.310 for atac-seq-pipeline (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`, recomputed from `cruxvault/results/t183/fastq_cost.tsv`). The finished rows ran from 2026-11-11 02:35 to 2026-11-13 08:14 UTC, about 54 h of wall time. The table build itself used 4.32 CPU-h. The working storage on Nibi `/scratch` was 240 GB of measurements, 944 GB of intermediate files and 772 GB of staged FASTQs. The frozen measurements are archived as 55 tar files of 242 GB (`cruxvault/results/t183/compute_and_storage.tsv`).==

==**Covariates and their limits.** The read-derived QC columns are sparse: 16.2% to 19.6% of training rows have NSC, RSC, NRF or PBC values, and no row has FRiP (`cruxvault/results/t183/qc_coverage.tsv`). Of the 249 training tracks present, 73.1% have reads of 36 bp or shorter, and all 7 blind testbed tracks are HiSeq 4000 runs with reads of 76 bp or longer (`cruxvault/results/t183/imbalance.tsv`). Counted from the served FASTQs, 43 of the 267 training experiments are paired-end, i.e. 16.1% (`cruxvault/results/t170/run_type_coverage.tsv`). The blind set is 100% paired-end, 100% HiSeq 4000 and 94% from one lab (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`). The run-type column of `imbalance.tsv` comes from the old store files, which undercount paired-end runs, so we do not quote it. The designed views imitate only two real shifts, paired-end to single-end and long to short reads. They cannot imitate platform, lab or the reverse directions (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`).==

### Methods: Evaluation

The figure of the protocol is `cruxvault/results/methods_data_eval/protocol_figure.svg`.

==**Split by tracks.** We use the EIC's own split (PI ruling 2). Models train on training tracks. Every score and every selection uses validation tracks. The blind tracks are read once, at the end (`cruxvault/INSTRUCTIONS/peeling_the_onion/long-horizon notepad.md`, ruling 2).==

==**Regions.** The evaluation set is frozen with sha256 `b5f7a4cf...0ce8` on the tables `a068a867...` (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`). It scores 256 regions of 16,384 bins each. A bin is 25 bp (`tools/onion_eval/ag2_peaks.py`, `BIN_BP`), so a region is 409,600 bp and the 256 regions hold 104.9 Mb. That is 256 x 16,384 / 113,251,272 = 3.7% of the valid bins of the genome mask (`cruxvault/results/t292/STRATA_REAL.md`). The regions are drawn at random with seed 0, and each has a valid-bin fraction of at least 0.9. A second, disjoint draw of 16 fit regions holds the bins on which the baselines fit their parameters (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`). By the training-peak frequency of their tiles, 1 region is in the class 0 to 0.05, 64 in 0.05 to 0.2, 124 in 0.2 to 0.5 and 67 above 0.5. No region is quiescent (`cruxvault/results/t292/STRATA_REAL.md`).==

==**Items and regimes.** An item is one validation target and its inputs. The set has 63 imputation items. In the leave-one-assay-out regime (43 items), the inputs are the base views of the same cell's training tracks of every other assay. In the accessibility-only regime (20 items), the only input is the cell's DNase-seq or ATAC-seq training track (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`; `tools/onion_eval/evalset.py`). No validation track is ever an input. An assay is imputation-scorable when it has at least 3 validation tracks with halves from at least 3 cells and at least 5 training tracks, and at least 2 validation tracks need a never-trained pair that is not a pure depth thinning (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`). Nine assays pass: H3K27ac, H3K27me3, H3K36me3, H3K4me1, H3K4me2, H3K4me3, H3K79me2, H3K9ac and H3K9me3. H3K9ac passes only through its replicate fallback, so a strict count is 8 (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`).==

==**Pairs.** A correction pair asks the model to turn a source view of a track into a target view of the same track. The set has 1,480 correction pairs over 11 correction-scorable assays, i.e. the 9 above plus H2AFZ and H4K20me1 (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`). A scored pair reads its source from the `pr1` half and its target from the `pr2` half, so the two sides share no read (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`). Headline metric 4 counts only never-trained pairs that are not pure depth thinnings. A never-trained pair is one of 18 directed view combinations that training never samples (for example `nodedup` to `mapq0`). Each validation track gets 2, 4 or 6 such pairs (38, 4 and 3 tracks) (`cruxvault/results/t170/withheld_design.md`, `cruxvault/results/t170/check.txt`). The set also has 3,134 replicate pairs, which measure the variance between replicates for metric 7 and for the variance layers (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`).==

==**The seven headline metrics** (`cruxvault/INSTRUCTIONS/peeling_the_onion/onion eval protocol.md`, section headline-metric-set). Each is quoted per assay as a macro average, with its seed spread.==
1. ==Recovered share of specific variance above the noise floor: 1 - (E_sp - N) / (V_sp - N), where E_sp is the cell-type-specific error, V_sp the specific truth variance and N the pseudoreplicate noise floor. It has two spaces: counts for selection, and the challenge's signal space for comparison with EIC entries (PI ruling 13).==
2. ==r_spec: the correlation between the truth and the prediction after a smooth function of the training average at each position is removed from each (`onion eval protocol.md`, section r-spec-specificity-correlation).==
3. ==Count CRPS skill against the per-position average: CRPS(average) - CRPS(model), with each CRPS split into its oracle-scaled and scale-error parts. CRPS is the continuous ranked probability score, i.e. an error of a full predictive distribution.==
4. ==Gap closed against depth-only scaling on never-trained pairs: (CRPS_depth-only - CRPS_model) / (CRPS_depth-only - CRPS_floor), where the floor predicts one pseudoreplicate half from the other (PI ruling 6).==
5. ==Depth-law error on never-trained pairs: the median of the absolute log2 ratio of the predicted total scale to the true depth ratio.==
6. ==PIT deviation, pooled and in the worst stratum: the mean absolute gap between the average non-randomized PIT curve and the diagonal on a 21-point grid. PIT is the probability integral transform, i.e. the predicted CDF at the observed count.==
7. ==Predicted over replicate variance: the predicted Negative Binomial variance divided by the variance between replicates, per replicate rung.==

==**Baselines.** Three baselines need no training (`tools/onion_eval/baselines.py`). The marginal Negative Binomial (NB) is one constant NB per assay. The per-position average is the mean rate over the training cells that measured the assay, leaving the target's cell out, scaled to the target's depth. Depth-only scaling multiplies the source counts by the depth ratio; it serves pairs only. The pseudoreplicate floor is the reference of metric 4, not a competitor. A model output is never blended with a baseline (decision D10). On the validation tracks, AlphaGenome 2 is an external comparison only. It trained on 32 validation tracks in 21 cells, and 12 validation tracks in 10 cells are unseen by both it and us (`cruxvault/results/t273/ag2_fair_sets.tsv`). Its outputs are never an input, a target or a selection signal.==

==**Composite and seed spread.** Each goal has one lead term on a 0 to 1 scale: metric 1 for imputation, metric 4 for correction, and 1 - PIT(arm) / PIT(marginal NB) for uncertainty. The composite is 0.5 imputation + 0.25 correction + 0.25 uncertainty, with each term clipped to [0, 1] (decision D13; PI ruling 18; `tools/onion_eval/select_rule.py`). The seed spread is the maximum minus the minimum across seeds, and a comparison uses the larger spread of its two arms (PI ruling 7). An arm beats the incumbent when its composite is higher by more than 2 seed spreads over at least 3 seeds and no goal drops by more than 2 of that goal's own spreads. Otherwise the simpler arm stays. The rule refuses to compare scores whose evaluation-set or table hashes differ, and it refuses a seed spread of exactly 0 (`cruxvault/INSTRUCTIONS/peeling_the_onion/long-horizon notepad.md`).==

==**Leak rules.** The dataset check enforces these with a count of 0 each: no validation or blind measurement in a training pair, no withheld combination in a training pair, no blind row readable in training or validation mode, and every testbed row flagged evaluation-only (`cruxvault/results/t183/DATASET_EIC_V1.md`). The evaluation set has 0 items, inputs and pairs on the blind split (`cruxvault/results/t185/SUBMITTED.md`). The NB sizes of the baselines and of the floor are fit on the fit regions, which never hold a scored bin (`tools/onion_eval/evalset.py`). Read-derived QC never enters the output-side covariates, because it would leak the target (decision D6). Training windows may overlap evaluation regions (PI ruling 39); the run measures the gap instead.==

==**The single blind pass.** The blind set is scored once, at the end, from its own evaluation set `eval/eic_v2/evalset_blind.json`. Each blind pass is written to a ledger, `BLIND_PASSES.tsv`, so a second look is visible and needs a stated reason. The final model for the EIC comparison trains on training plus validation tracks, as the challenge entrants did (PI ruling 21). Iterations 1 to 3 read no blind track.==

==**Two scoring tiers.** A settled score runs each pair on all 256 regions, or on the smallest of 8, 16 or 32 nested regions that passes a check written before any model was scored (`cruxvault/results/pair_region_check/PAIR_CHECK_PREREGISTRATION.md`). A screening score runs pairs on 16 nested regions, and items keep all 256 regions. Each table states its tier. No selection is settled from a screening score. The baselines of iteration 1 are settled scores.==
