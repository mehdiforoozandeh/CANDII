# f9 — Exploratory readings the PI has not judged, and the depth and run-type tests…

Parent:: [[q1_do_the_recorded_experimental_covariates_]]

## Answer

Partly. The covariates carry information: on trained pairs a covariate-conditioned map beats its scrambled twin almost everywhere and beats one-warp on depth and run type. But no design tested learns a rule that transfers: on never-trained arm → arm pairs the gain shrinks and no design meets the depth law, although the real data obey it within 0.71%.
