# f162 — Leg A, redone: the read-start model's count-space overconfidence was a…

Parent:: [[q94_is_the_read_start_or_the_read_count_form]]

## Answer

Leg A, redone: the read-start model's count-space overconfidence was a conversion artifact. The covariance term in the start-to-count conversion moved its calibration ratio from 1.258 to 1.032 (22-assay macro, 3 seeds, even regions; reference 1.064). Like-for-like against the equally recalibrated reference, the difference (-0.029 all assays, +0.047 scorable) is inside the seed range (0.048, 0.060). Calibration therefore no longer separates the two formulations; skill and leg B decide.
