## Answer

On trained pairs g beats its no-covariates twin in counts for all four read-selection knobs (median gain 0.08–0.18 against seed wobble 0.003–0.06) and less in p (0.03–0.14); on never-trained pairs the p gain vanishes or turns negative. One-warp already closes about half the gap for duplicate removal, and almost none for the MAPQ filter, whose effect is regional.
