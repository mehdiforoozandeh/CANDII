Parent:: [[q5_does_a_depth_offset_on_f_the_source_dept]]

## Answer

The offset is a calibration fix for the simplest design, not a general accuracy fix, and it does not lock the depth law in. It clears against the capacity grid only for design A (DNase CRPS 1.247 ± 0.016 against 3.06; 6 of 54 cells clear, all A-type) and in 0 of 36 cells for B–D2. The depth law is met in 0 of 72 cells, while the no-covariates twins of D nearly meet it (0.054–0.135), so g bends the law when it reads the covariates.
