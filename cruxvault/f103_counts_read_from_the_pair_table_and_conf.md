# f103 — Counts read from the pair table and confirmed by an independent reviewer; no…

Parent:: [[q58_can_the_second_experiments_separate_a_la]]

## Answer

Not per assay class. Of 71 built second experiments (35 other lab, 36 same lab), pairs from another lab with the same donor reach 8 pairs from 2 cell types in broad histone marks, 6 from 2 in narrow marks and 2 from 2 in DNase-seq, under the rule of 5 pairs from 3 cell types. Lab and donor changed together are estimable: 13 pairs from 4 cell types (broad) and 6 from 4 (narrow). Pooled over assay classes the same-donor class has 16 pairs from 3 cell types, which is judged not sound because the classes differ in signal structure. The 17 unbuilt DNase-seq rows would add only 2 such pairs.
