# f403 — Wide-grid recalibration (n divided by a per-assay factor) made the…

Parent:: [[q90_can_a_count_space_model_like_minicandi_b]]

## Answer

Wide-grid recalibration (n divided by a per-assay factor) made the expected-p validation scores worse, not better: mse rose on 7 of 8 tracks versus the raw copy in the same mode (H3K4me3 most: C02M22 9.5 -> 16.1, C20M22 3.2 -> 6.5, C45M22 3.2 -> 4.5; C24M25 fell 11.48 -> 11.37). Combined rank of 11 worsened on 4 (C10M17 1 -> 2, C45M22 1 -> 2, C02M22 4.3 -> 4.5, C16M17 4 -> 4.1) and improved on none. Single seed, 8 experiments.
