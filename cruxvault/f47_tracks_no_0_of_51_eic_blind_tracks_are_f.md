# f47 — Tracks: no. 0 of 51 EIC blind tracks are Flashzoi training experiments, at…

Parent:: [[q28_did_flashzoi_train_on_eic_blind_tracks_o]]

## Answer

Tracks: no. 0 of 51 EIC blind tracks are Flashzoi training experiments, at the same-experiment level and at the same-biosample-and-assay level (two independent methods agree); 44 of 45 validation and 252 of 267 training tracks are. Loci: yes. The training folds of all 4 Flashzoi replicates cover 68.95% of chr1-22,X as target windows (test fold 3: 11.40%). Under PI ruling 26 Flashzoi is an ablation arm only, never in the final model, and its arm is judged on fold-3 loci only.
