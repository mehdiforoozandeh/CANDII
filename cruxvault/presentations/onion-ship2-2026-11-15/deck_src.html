Recapthe task and the three headline metrics

One model imputes a missing assay, corrects a track to other processing settings, and states how sure it is

Imputation

Specific variance recovered above noise: the share of the cell-type-specific variance of a held-out track that the prediction explains, after the read-sampling noise of the truth (the noise floor) is removed. Zero: no cell-type signal. One: all that is recoverable.

Covariate correction

Gap closed: where the model's correction error (CRPS, the error of a whole predictive distribution) sits between depth-only scaling (zero) and the oracle floor (one), on pairs of processing settings that training never sees.

Calibrated uncertainty

Uncertainty term: one minus the model's PIT deviation over that of the marginal negative binomial (NB). PIT is the predicted cumulative probability at the observed count. Zero: as calibrated as one constant NB per track. Below zero: worse.

The three terms are the lead metrics of the three goals. Each is a macro average over the scorable assays. Each model value is a mean over training seeds, with the seed range, that is, the maximum minus the minimum over seeds. A difference counts when it is larger than the larger seed range of its two arms; the selection rule asks for more than twice that range on the composite.

Methodsthe new scale of gap closed (PI ruling 47)

With the oracle floor as the top of the scale, every model lands between depth-only scaling and one

    The old top of the scale was the half-against-half floor: it predicts the second read half from the first one, so it carries one half's sampling noise, and smooth predictors went past it. The oracle floor is half the mean absolute difference of the target's two read halves; no predictor that sees only the source can expect to beat it on clean pairs. On pairs where one side keeps duplicate reads, source and target can share DNA fragments, so the oracle is not a strict bound there. The composite uses the all-pairs value; the clean-pair value is reported beside it.

    Results(a) the ladder of six architectures, three goal terms

    Every architecture beats the position average; rungs two and three lead correction and rung four leads imputation

      The ladder holds six architectures of about one million parameters, each trained on the same number of windows: pointwise, convolutional autoencoder, convolutional autoencoder with attention, U-Net, axial, and Perceiver. Each dot is one training seed; the bar is the seed mean. The dashed line is the training-free reference: the position average on the first two panels, and the marginal negative binomial at zero on the third.

      Results(b) the ladder: composite and compute

        Panel a: the composite per seed. The composite weighs imputation by one half and correction and uncertainty by one quarter each, after each term is clipped to the range from zero to one. Panel b: the training compute that each run recorded, in floating-point operations, on a log scale. The pointwise rung ran on a GPU and the context rungs on CPUs, so wall time is not compared.

        Results(c) the ladder: imputation against correction

        No architecture leads both goals: rung four buys imputation with correction

          Each point is one seed of one rung, placed by its imputation term and its correction term. Up and to the right is better on both. Why the U-Net trades one goal for the other is an open question in the notebook; it decides which rungs the ablations of iteration two take.

          Results(d) the wiring checks of the pointwise rung

          No wiring change moves the pointwise rung's composite beyond the seed range; scrambling the covariates costs imputation

            Each row is one arm minus the base, per training seed, with the same seed on both sides. Dots are the seeds, the diamond is the mean, and the grey band is plus and minus the larger seed range of the two arms. A diamond outside the band exceeds the seed range. An arm with one seed has no range and is not judged. Switching off the DNA input changes nothing measurable at this rung, which sees one bin at a time.

            Results(e) the rung-4 arms

            At rung four, scrambling the covariates raises gap closed on all pairs and lowers it on the clean pairs; hiding depth costs calibration

              Same layout as the previous slide, one column per goal term. Clean pairs are the headline pairs where neither side keeps duplicate reads; on the other pairs, source and target can share DNA fragments. The two gap-closed columns average over different sets of assays, so they are not a strict split of each other.

              Results(f) depth hidden from the network (the hard offset, PI ruling 48)

                In both arms the predicted mean is the depth times two to the power eta, where eta is the network's depth-free log rate. The hard-offset arm only removes log depth from the covariates that the network reads. Panel a: the uncertainty term per seed. Panel b: the composite difference split into the three weighted, clipped terms. Why calibration collapses is an open question in the notebook; the first suspect is the predicted negative binomial size, which may need depth.

                Results(g) training-region selection at a limited budget

                No selection rule beats uniform tiling; two lose on the composite, through the uncertainty term

                  Each arm trains rung four on the same small budget of windows, drawn by one rule: a tilt toward high candidate cis-regulatory element density or high signal variance, with or without a loss weight that keeps the objective unbiased; k-center, which spreads windows out; or a draw from a fixed set of territories, the control for k-center. The reference is uniform tiling.

                  Results(h) AlphaGenome 2 on the tracks that neither it nor we trained on

                    The metric is the recalibrated specificity correlation in the challenge's signal space: the correlation with the truth after both lose the training mean, with a per-track monotone recalibration. The noise floor compares two read halves at half depth each, so it is not a ceiling for a prediction. Our model has no row yet; it needs its predictions turned into the signal space.

                    Results(i) the variance layers in the depth-free eta space

                    Above read sampling, the layers do not change with depth in eta space; on log counts they do

                      Eta is the log of the rate per read: the mean count is the depth times the exponent of eta. In that space read sampling is a known term that can be subtracted, and every layer above it, replicate and cell type, is free of depth. Each line thins every pair to a half and a quarter of the common depth. On log counts the cell-type share falls as depth falls, because the sampling layer grows; in eta space the cell-type layer stays flat.
