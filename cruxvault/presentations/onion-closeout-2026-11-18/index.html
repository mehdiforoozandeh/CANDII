Methodshow to read every number in this deck

Each model is scored on imputation, correction and calibration; a difference counts only when it is beyond the seed range

Imputation

Variance recovered: the share of the cell-type-specific variance of a held-out track that the prediction explains, above the read-sampling noise floor. All regions.

Correction

Gap closed on the never-trained crop pairs: zero is depth-only scaling, one is the oracle reference (the expected error of read sampling alone).

Calibration

One minus the model's PIT deviation over that of one constant Negative Binomial per track. PIT: the predicted cumulative probability at the observed count.

Composite

imputation + correction + calibration, each clipped to zero to one. Beside it: the correction-only term, the share of the covariate cost a model closes against its own same-view score.

  • Two tiers. Imputation and calibration use all regions. The two correction terms use pair regions: a screening score, not a settled one.
  • Significance criterion. Every value is a mean over seeds with its seed range (maximum minus minimum). "Beyond" means the difference exceeds the larger seed range; the selection rule asks for twice that range.

The seed range is the project's seed statistic; no standard deviation is computed. The composite is the selection metric; the correction-only term is reported beside it because a model that ignores its input also scores well on gap closed. The correction scores rest on few pairs, so their seed ranges are wide.

(1) Data(a) the augmented EIC tables partial

Of training measurements, are base views and are counterfactual views of the same experiments

  • The first freeze wrote rows: training, validation and blind (evaluation-only) rows. Every track was rebuilt from its reads with the standard ENCODE pipeline of its assay.
  • A counterfactual view is the same reads processed another way (thinned, no duplicate removal, no mapping-quality filter, single-end, shorter reads). Read-level views are rare (), because most training reads are already short.

The counts are for the table version with filled covariates, which is the default now. The views imitate only two real shifts, paired-end to single-end and long to short reads; they cannot imitate platform or lab.

(2) Objective(a) what miniCANDI is trained to do delivered

One loss trains three goals: a Negative Binomial likelihood on hidden tracks, other views and hidden bins

  • The same-view target counts only on its hidden bins, so the model cannot copy its input. The withheld view pairs are never drawn in training; they are the pairs that the correction score uses.
  • Open choices are marked in the document: the kind weights, the mask fractions and the training views wait for the rescoring and the mix-and-match round.

The objective document traces every formula and default to a file and line of the code; the code is the authority. With every switch at its default, the total loss equals the plain Negative Binomial negative log-likelihood, bit for bit, and a test checks this.

No loss arm beats the Negative Binomial likelihood on two or more models

  • The one clear gain: imputation weight four raises conv + attention imputation by (range ), beyond twice the range, on one model only. It enters the conv + attention mix-and-match pool.
  • CRPS (continuous ranked probability score, an error of a whole distribution) as a loss and a Poisson mean change no term on the conv autoencoder or the U-Net.

A filled dot is beyond the larger seed range; an open dot is within it. The composite is a screening score because it holds the correction term; imputation uses all regions. The rescoring of the imputation-weight arm decides whether it stays in the pool.

(3) Evaluation(a) can a few pair regions stand in for all of them? partial

The pre-registered check failed at every subset size, so correction terms must be scored on all regions

  • The check (pre-registered: fixed in writing before any run) used runs of arms and region-bootstrap draws. At pair regions, condition one passed , and only of the run-by-assay cells sat inside their interval.
  • The selection verdicts did not change at any size (condition three). The levels and the seed ranges of the correction terms did.

The amendment of the sixteenth put the corrected correction terms into the check before any score on all regions existed. The consequence is the tier rule of this deck: every ablation effect on a correction term is a screening result until the decisive arms are rescored on all regions.

(4) Design(e) what a scaling session starts from

The three base configs are the recipe; scaling changes the parameter budget, the windows, the context and the data

  • Recipe. The base config of each model equals the mix-and-match reference, key for key; every design-table value that a config key sets agrees with it.
  • Training length is not settled. No architecture converged at windows, and twice the windows lowered the held-out loss but not the composite.
  • Correction needs all regions. Each scoring of one run-seed costs tens of CPU-hours and a large prediction store on scratch.
  • Blind shift. Blind tracks may differ in platform and lab; a value seen only in blind tracks has no learned embedding.
  • Records. A run's own record does not hold its dataset and loss defaults; a scaled run should record them.

The full risk list is in section four of the design document. The fixed input depth gain is deployment-unrealistic and must hold under native-depth scoring before it enters the recipe.
